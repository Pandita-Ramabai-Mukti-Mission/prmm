Founded in 1889 by Pandita Ramabai as a home for widows, Mukti Mission — "Mukti" meaning freedom, liberation and salvation — has grown into 14 ministries serving orphaned, destitute and vulnerable women and children, regardless of caste, creed or religion.

### Mukti's Vision

Christ Centred Home where destitute women and children irrespective of their background are accepted...

### Mukti's Mission

We seek, in the Spirit of Christ, to sow seeds of change in the lives of every individual under the care of Mukti Mission...

### Basis of Mukti
