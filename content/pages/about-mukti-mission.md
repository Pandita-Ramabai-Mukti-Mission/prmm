---
title: About Mukti Mission
---

Founded in 1889 by Pandita Ramabai as a home for widows, Mukti Mission — "Mukti" meaning freedom, liberation and salvation — has grown into 14 ministries serving orphaned, destitute and vulnerable women and children, regardless of caste, creed or religion. Mukti has continued that work ever since Ramabai's death in 1922, now in its 130th-plus year, with a stated passion of "creating beauty out of ashes."
