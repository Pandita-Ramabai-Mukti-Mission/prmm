title: Pandita Ramabai
---

23 April 1858 &ndash; 5 April 1922

In the late 1800s, Ramabai Dongre-Medhavi founded a home for widows that would grow into Mukti Mission — "Mukti" meaning freedom, liberation and salvation. For over 130 years the mission she started has served orphaned, destitute and disabled women and children regardless of caste, creed or religion.
