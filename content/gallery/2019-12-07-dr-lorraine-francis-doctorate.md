Pandita Ramabai Mukti Mission's (PRMM) Mission Director, Dr. Lorraine Francis, was conferred the Hon. Doctorate by Dr. Sam Kumar, founder of UCM Bible University. On 29th November, a special ceremony for the occasion was held in Mumbai, where Dr. Lorraine was honored with the Hon. Doctorate.

Dr. Lorraine has labored at PRMM for more than 30 years. This recognition reflects the heart and labor she has put into her calling — the outline of her thesis is "Tending, Mending and Bending."
