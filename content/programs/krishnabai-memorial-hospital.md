Krishnabai Memorial Hospital was founded in 1948 to serve both Mukti's own residents and the wider community around Kedgaon — care aimed particularly at families who could not otherwise afford treatment. Renovation and pharmacy improvements are ongoing, with a long-term goal of growing capacity well beyond its current 30 beds.
