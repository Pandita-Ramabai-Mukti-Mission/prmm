The Mission's work with blind and special-needs children dates back to the famine years of the 1890s, when the first children arrived needing dedicated teaching methods. A purpose-built Special Needs School opened on the Mukti campus in 2013, sited alongside the Mission's other schools so children can learn and take part alongside their peers.

The section continues to look for trained staff and volunteers to support its work.
