Agape Bible Institute is a theology college established in 2011 for people called to ministry but unable to leave their families or jobs for full-time theological study. It now runs classes across eight centers in Maharashtra — including Mumbai, Pune, Nashik and Kedgaon — with three batches already having graduated from its Mukti Mission campus.
