The Mission's care for the blind traces back to the 1890s famine years, when the first children needing this kind of support arrived at Kedgaon. Bartimi Sadan continues that work today as a residential home, alongside a school, for blind and visually special-needs women and children — with an ongoing need for trained staff and volunteers.
