Manorama Memorial Girls' High School has served the Kedgaon area for close to five decades under the motto "Blessed to be a blessing." At a time when girls in the area were often married off once they reached secondary school, the school's continued counselling and guidance helped shift that expectation — the more recent addition of a junior college is a direct result, giving girls a path to further study rather than an early marriage.
