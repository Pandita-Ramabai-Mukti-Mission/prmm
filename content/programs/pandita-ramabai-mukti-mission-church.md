The church's cornerstone was laid in 1899, inscribed with Zechariah 4:6 — "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, says the Lord." Built in the shape of a cross from local stone and teak shipped from Burma, the building seats around 2,500 people and still stands on its original wooden-pegged flooring, requiring very little upkeep well over a century on.
