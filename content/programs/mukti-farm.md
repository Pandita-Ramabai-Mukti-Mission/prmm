Land that lay unused for decades has been restored well by well, field by field — wells cleared and tested, fencing installed against encroachment, and a portable generator solving what had long been an irrigation problem. Sugarcane, wheat, jowar, maize and a large vegetable garden now grow across the Mission's acreage, managed by Farm Manager Ishwar Dass, who has also brought back training from a regenerative-farming scholarship in Western Australia to apply at Kedgaon.
