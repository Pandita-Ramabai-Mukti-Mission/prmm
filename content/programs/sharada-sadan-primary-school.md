Sharada Sadan began on 11 March 1889, named for its first student. Within its first years it had grown from a handful of students to dozens of widows and unmarried girls, combining general and vocational education — Ramabai is credited as the first to introduce kindergarten-style education in India, and Sharada Sadan as the first school to offer it.

The school now teaches children from the Mission as well as from Kedgaon and neighbouring villages, with students sitting the Maharashtra State Scholarship exams and a 100% pass record.
