Started in 1993 with just 33 students on the Mission campus, the school relocated in 2000 to its present site amid Mukti's farmland. It has since grown to around 800 students, taught by a staff of 25-plus, under the motto "By love serve each other."

The school's focus is on drawing out each child's own potential rather than teaching to a syllabus alone — treating education as the foundation for every other opportunity a child will have later in life.
