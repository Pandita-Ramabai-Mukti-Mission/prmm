Priti Sadan (Home of Love) traces back to the famine relief effort of 1896–97, when Pandita Ramabai brought hundreds of starving women and children to the farm she had purchased at Kedgaon. As the community grew, residents were housed by age and need — older women at Priti Sadan, blind women and children at Bartimi and Krupa Sadan — a structure that has continued for well over a century.
