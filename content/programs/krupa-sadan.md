Krupa Sadan grew out of the same 1896–97 famine relief effort that brought hundreds of women and children to Kedgaon and led to the Mission's first permanent building, "The Barn," in 1898. As the community was organised by age and need, Krupa Sadan became one of the homes caring for blind women and children alongside Bartimi Sadan.
